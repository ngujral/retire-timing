Retire Timing

Privacy

Retire Timing has no accounts and no server. Everything you enter, and every result, stays in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Your entries (birth year and month, pension amounts, balances and choices) are saved in your browser’s local storage so they’re there when you come back. They never leave the device. Downloads you make (the CSV table or a PDF) are created on your device.

Deleting your data

Tap Start over (clear my data) at the bottom of the calculator, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

What we don’t do

No sign-up, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and no sharing or selling of data. The numbers you type are never sent anywhere.

Third parties

The page loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives a normal web request (your IP address and browser) when the font is downloaded. No calculator inputs are included in that request. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles the request for the page itself.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, the referring site, country, and browser and device type. It uses no cookies, doesn’t follow you across sites and doesn’t identify you, and it never receives anything you type into the calculator.
